# Deol Build Brand Launch

design the landing page for a construction company based in australia named "Punjab Homes" now they are rebranding themselves as "Deol Build". As of now they are working from last 15 years and have delivered around 500+ projects

Focus -
1. Premium design
2. Borderless
3. Appropriate use of negative space
4. Proper text sizes for headings and body
5. Background colors should be light but neither same nor pure white - they should be section wise

Instructions - 
1. A light theme website - build with user journey to gain trust - should proper use of images and videos not entirely flooded with text
2. Home page should have sections (header, landing, stats, philosophy, process of work(how quality maintained at every stage), services, featured projects, testimonials, FAQs, Contact section (query form), Footer

Section wise vision
1. Header (logo and menu icon) -header should floating over landing section and logo should not visible until scroll, on click of menu icon full page drop down should come and options should listed with numbers in left section one by one
2. Landing section - high quality video along with our logo on top of it
3. Philosophy section should oriented towards client first and budget friendly and best quality and every project better than previous

Note make sure to use icons, images, negative space and videos more as compared to text
keep icons font very light
